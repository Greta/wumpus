How to play

Find the wumpus, then take aim and fire into its room. You have just one arrow, so make it count.

  • Use the direction pad, arrow keys, or number pad (8, 2, 4, 6) to move.
  • Tap Aim or press F, then choose a direction to shoot. Tap again or press Escape to cancel.
  • Blood means the wumpus is within two connected rooms. Slime warns of a nearby pit. Bats may carry you somewhere unexpected.
  • Tunnels bend, and the cave wraps around at its edges. Dark rooms are still unexplored.
  • After a hunt, view the full map with M or try again with Enter. Your score lasts for this visit.
